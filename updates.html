✨ Orbits

12 Jul 2026, 12:04 Posted by FLAME

📣 This is the new Safe currency we have been working on for a while to make sure it's ready for release!

  • 1 Orbit = 100,000 Cookies
  • Orbits Cannot be robbed or heisted
  • To convert from Cookies to Orbits, use the command: ?convert {amount} orbit, and it will transfer from your jar to Orbits
  • The cookies Jar is always separate from Orbits, but your total amount of cookies is affected when you use the ?bal command
  • Orbits Do Not affect roles like Richest of the Week; it's only impacted by the Cookies in your jar

    Ex: having so many Orbits in balance won't grant Richest of the Week, you need to have cookies instead
  • 🛒 Orbits can be spent or bought in #shop channel so far
  • So far, there is no way to convert back from Orbits to Cookies, but this part might get updated; we will let you know!
  • I hope you enjoyed this quick, dedicated guide. For any questions, you know where to ask
